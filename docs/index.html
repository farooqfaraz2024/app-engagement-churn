Why users rank as risky

Each user gets up to three plain-language reasons. They explain the ranking; they do not prove that acting on a reason will change the outcome.

What drives churn

Feature importance is the drop in ROC-AUC when a feature is shuffled. Silence dominates: the longer since a user was last active, the less likely they return.
